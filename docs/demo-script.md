## 1. 90-Second Rapid Pitch Script

*(Target time: 1 minute 30 seconds — ideal for quick judging rounds or stage presentations)*

> "Respected judges, during catastrophic floods in Pakistan, emergency queues fail because of one fundamental flaw: they only prioritize who shouts loudest with a smartphone.
>
> Meanwhile, the most vulnerable—elderly residents living alone, low-income informal settlements along riverbanks, and people whose cell towers have washed away—become completely invisible.
>
> We built **Nigehbaan (نگہبان)** to solve Problem 01: finding who needs help most.
>
> Nigehbaan introduces three major tactical breakthroughs:
> 1. **The Uncertainty Radar:** When an entire sector goes dark during rising floodwaters, our system doesn't wait for calls. It detects the silence and automatically synthesizes a high-priority incident for an immediate physical welfare recon sweep.
> 2. **Deterministic, Auditable Triage:** Lives cannot depend on an LLM hallucinating a priority score. Our scoring is 100% deterministic multi-criteria math, producing an explainable 0 to 100 score where missing data never penalizes the victim.
> 3. **Tactical Opportunity Cost Warnings:** Before a commander dispatches a scarce shallow-draft boat, Nigehbaan warns them: *'Assigning this boat leaves 0 boats in Sector 2, delaying the next queued dialysis patient by +35 minutes.'*
>
> Nigehbaan ensures that in the chaos of disaster, no human life is left invisible."

## 2. 3-Minute Walkthrough Script (100% Tier-1-Only)

> **Resilience Rule:** This 3-minute presentation script covers **Tier 1 core features exclusively**. If Tier 2 features are cut due to deadline constraints, this demonstration script will still execute from start to finish with zero missing steps or dead ends.

### Step 1: Hook & Tactical HUD Overview (0:00 – 0:30)
- **Action:** Open `https://nigehbaan.vercel.app` (or local preview) at baseline $T-0$.
- **Script:**
  > "Welcome to Nigehbaan. In flood disasters, first-come first-served triage fails: the system only hears people with functioning smartphones who shout loudest. Meanwhile, trapped elderly residents, submerged informal settlements, and people in dead zones are left invisible.
  > 
  > Nigehbaan is an operational decision-support platform designed to find the people the system can't hear. Here is our District Alpha tactical HUD: live GIS map, priority queue, uncertainty radar, and timeline scrubber. All displayed data is strictly simulated."

### Step 2: Deterministic Scorer & Explainability Card (0:30 – 1:00)
- **Action:** Advance scrubber to $T+3$ $\to$ Click top incident **`NG-101`** (Score 86.5 — Critical).
- **Script:**
  > "Notice our top-priority emergency: NG-101, a hemodialysis patient trapped in chest-deep water in Sector 2.
  > 
  > This priority score of 86.5 is NOT generated by an LLM. It is calculated by a deterministic 5-factor mathematical formula. Commanders see the transparent breakdown right here: Life Threat 4.5, Medical Urgency 5.0, Evacuation Inability 4.0. If any factor is missing, our engine renormalizes weights rather than unfairly penalizing a caller with a zero."

### Step 3: Uncertainty Radar & Conflicting Claims (1:00 – 1:30)
- **Action:** Advance scrubber to $T+6$ $\to$ Point to the **Uncertainty Radar** panel on the dashboard.
- **Script:**
  > "Notice the Uncertainty Radar detecting real-world noise:
  > - Two callers submitted conflicting reports on the Main Highway Bridge: NG-108 claims the bridge is open, while NG-109 reports it is washed out. Nigehbaan flags them side-by-side for human verification without auto-merging.
  > - Over in Sector 2, NG-112 has a screaming caller but null coordinates—flagged as Missing Location so teams don't dispatch blind."

### Step 4: Sector Blind-Spot Synthesis (1:30 – 2:15)
- **Action:** Point to Sector 3A (*Riverbank Settlement*) on the map and queue at $T+6$.
- **Script:**
  > "Here is our core breakthrough: Sector Blind-Spot Detection. At 08:55, Tower-Alpha-3 collapsed. For 110 minutes, Sector 3A has been completely silent while the flood gauge reached 1.9 meters.
  > 
  > Instead of ignoring the silence, Nigehbaan's blind-spot monitor detects the blackout and synthesizes Incident **`NG-103`**, enforced at score 68.7 (High Priority), labeled *'Physical Welfare Recon / Field Sweep'*.
  > 
  > The map polygon highlights in warning tangerine with a dashed border. This tells dispatchers: don't wait for calls that will never come—send an amphibious recon sweep now."

### Step 5: Tactical Opportunity Cost & Human Approval (2:15 – 2:45)
- **Action:** Click **`NG-101`** in the queue $\to$ Click **"Assign Resource"** $\to$ Select **`RES-01` (Shallow Jet-Boat 01)**.
- **Script:**
  > "When a commander assigns Shallow Jet-Boat 01 to NG-101, Nigehbaan calculates the deterministic Opportunity Cost before commitment.
  > 
  > The system alerts: *'Committing Shallow Jet-Boat 01 leaves 0 shallow-draft assets available in Sector 2. Queued incident NG-104 [Score 56.5 — High] will incur an estimated +35 minute delay.'*
  > 
  > Crucially, Nigehbaan never autonomously dispatches assets. It arms human coordinators with consequences. The coordinator clicks **Approve Assignment**, and an immutable audit log entry is recorded."

### Step 6: Honest AI Fallback & 1-Click Simulation Reset (2:45 – 3:00)
- **Action:** Open Report modal $\to$ point to fallback label $\to$ click **"Reset Simulation"** in header.
- **Script:**
  > "If Gemini API connectivity drops, our deterministic heuristic parser takes over, openly labeled *'Offline heuristic extraction (not AI)'*—honest AI with zero deception.
  > 
  > And with one click on 'Reset Simulation', the entire District Alpha scenario restores to its pristine baseline in under 5 milliseconds. Nigehbaan: leaving no one invisible. Thank you."
